Problems with LLM:

- No source (references, evidence, citations).
- Out-of-date information.
- Limited information.
- Hard to adapt to domain-specific knowledge.

Benefits of RAG:

- No need to retrain the model to update information. Retraining is expensive and time-consuming.
- Access to domain-specific knowledge, instead of relying on information learned during training.
